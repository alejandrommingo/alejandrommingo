# Alejandro Martínez-Mingo

**Psychology · Cognitive science · Computational models of language**

I am a *Profesor Ayudante Doctor* (Assistant Professor) in the Department of Behavioral Sciences Methodology, Faculty of Psychology, at the Universidad Nacional de Educación a Distancia (UNED), Spain.

My research focuses on the representation of meaning, semantic geometry and conceptual subspaces, quantum-inspired cognitive models, and the psychological validity of measurements derived from language models. I also work on computational methodology, data analysis, and psychometrics.

**Academic website:** https://alejandrommingo.github.io/

### Research and code

- [QLang](https://github.com/alejandrommingo/QLang): tools for contextual semantic representations and subspace analysis.
- [QLSA](https://github.com/alejandrommingo/QLSA) and [QSub](https://github.com/alejandrommingo/QSub): exploratory work on conceptual subspaces.
- [AGRUPA](https://github.com/alejandrommingo/AGRUPA): text-processing and dictionary-based measures for descriptions of artworks.
- [SampleSizeApp](https://github.com/alejandrommingo/SampleSizeApp): sample-size calculation for quantitative research.

Research questions, publications and related software: **[Research page](https://alejandrommingo.github.io/en/research/)**.
